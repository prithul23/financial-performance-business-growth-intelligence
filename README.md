# Financial Performance & Business Growth Intelligence System

### End-to-End Business Intelligence | Financial Analytics | Data-Driven Decision Making

---

## 1. Project Overview

The **Financial Performance & Business Growth Intelligence System** is an end-to-end Business Intelligence project developed to evaluate an organisation's financial performance, monitor operational efficiency, identify financial risks, and support strategic business decisions.

The project transforms structured financial data into meaningful business insights through data preparation, PostgreSQL database management, SQL-based business analysis, interactive Tableau dashboards, and management reporting.

The analysis covers key business functions, including revenue, expenses, profitability, client performance, departmental spending, budget utilisation, invoices, payments, and outstanding receivables.

**Project Objective:** To develop an integrated Business Intelligence solution that converts financial data into actionable insights and supports effective financial planning, performance monitoring, and management decision-making.

## 2. Business Problem

Organisations generate financial data across multiple business functions. Without proper data integration and analysis, management may face challenges in understanding financial performance, monitoring expenditure, evaluating client contributions, and managing outstanding payments.

This project addresses the following business questions:

- What is the overall financial performance of the organisation?
- How do revenue and expenses change over time?
- What is the organisation's operating profit and profit margin?
- Which clients contribute the most to total revenue?
- Which departments and expense categories account for major spending?
- How effectively are allocated budgets being utilised?
- What is the current invoice and payment position?
- How much revenue remains outstanding?
- Which receivables require immediate collection attention?
- What actions can improve financial performance and operational control?

## 8. Business Recommendations

Based on the analytical findings, the project proposes the following management actions:

1. **Receivables Management:** Establish structured collection follow-ups and assign responsibility for outstanding accounts.
2. **Ageing Risk Reduction:** Prioritise long-outstanding invoices and introduce escalation procedures.
3. **Budget Monitoring:** Conduct regular budget-versus-actual reviews to identify significant spending variances.
4. **Client Diversification:** Monitor client revenue concentration and strengthen the overall client portfolio.
5. **Expense Optimisation:** Review major expense categories and departmental spending patterns.
6. **Financial Performance Monitoring:** Introduce regular KPI reporting to support management decisions.

A proposed 30–60–90-day action plan is included in the project results to translate these recommendations into practical management activities.

## 11. Data Quality & Limitations

The project uses a synthetic financial dataset created for business intelligence and portfolio demonstration purposes.

Data validation identified missing financial values and differences between certain SQL and Tableau KPI calculations.

These issues are documented as data-quality and reconciliation considerations. The reported financial figures should therefore be interpreted within the scope of the relevant analysis and its calculation methodology.

The project does not represent audited financial statements or actual confidential company records.

## 12. Conclusion

The Financial Performance & Business Growth Intelligence System demonstrates how raw financial data can be transformed into structured business intelligence through data preparation, relational database management, SQL analysis, advanced analytical techniques, and interactive visualisation.
